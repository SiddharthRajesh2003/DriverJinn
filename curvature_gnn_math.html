DriverJinn — Curvature-Aware GNN

Mathematical Formulation  ·  Cancer Driver Gene Prediction

1
Ollivier-Ricci Curvature

For each edge $(u, v)$ in the gene interaction network, curvature quantifies local geometric stress via the transport cost between neighborhood distributions.

Edge Curvature
$$\kappa(u,v) \;=\; 1 \;-\; \frac{W_1(\mu_u,\, \mu_v)}{d(u,v)}$$

$W_1$: 1-Wasserstein (earth mover's) distance  ·  $\mu_v$: uniform distribution over neighbors of $v$  ·  $d(u,v)$: shortest-path distance

Curvature Feature Augmentation (per node)
$$\tilde{x}_v \;=\; \Bigl[\, x_v \;\Big\|\; \bar{\kappa}_v,\; \kappa_v^{\min},\; \kappa_v^{\max} \,\Bigr] \;\in\; \mathbb{R}^{d\,+\,15}$$

Three curvature statistics appended to the original $d$-dimensional node features. Total input dim: $d + 15$ (e.g., $58 + 15 = 73$).

2
Schur Complement Graph Augmentation

Two stochastic graph views are constructed by eliminating a random $\rho$-fraction of nodes via Schur complement reduction of the graph Laplacian.

Block Partition of Graph Laplacian
$$L \;=\; \begin{bmatrix} L_{AA} & L_{AB} \\ L_{BA} & L_{BB} \end{bmatrix}$$

$A$: retained nodes  ·  $B$: eliminated nodes (random $\rho = 0.2$ sample)

Schur Complement onto Retained Set A
$$L^{(S)} \;=\; L_{AA} \;-\; L_{AB}\, L_{BB}^{-1}\, L_{BA}$$
Two Independent Views
$$\tilde{G}_1,\; \tilde{G}_2 \;\longleftarrow\; \text{two independent random draws of } B \text{ at } \rho = 0.2$$
3
Multi-Head Graph Attention Encoder (GAT)

Applied independently to each view. Produces per-node embeddings via learned attention over neighbor features.

Attention Coefficient (layer $\ell$, head $k$)
$$\alpha_{vu}^{(k,\ell)} \;=\; \frac{ \exp\!\Bigl(\operatorname{LeakyReLU}\!\bigl(\mathbf{a}^k \cdot [W^k h_v^{(\ell-1)} \;\|\; W^k h_u^{(\ell-1)}]\bigr)\Bigr) }{ \displaystyle\sum_{w \in \mathcal{N}(v)} \exp\!\Bigl(\operatorname{LeakyReLU}\!\bigl(\mathbf{a}^k \cdot [W^k h_v^{(\ell-1)} \;\|\; W^k h_w^{(\ell-1)}]\bigr)\Bigr) }$$
Node Update (multi-head concatenation)
$$h_v^{(\ell)} \;=\; \Big\|_{k=1}^{K}\; \sigma\!\left( \sum_{u \in \mathcal{N}(v)} \alpha_{vu}^{(k,\ell)}\; W^k h_u^{(\ell-1)} \right)$$

$K = 4$ attention heads  ·  hidden dim $= 128$  ·  dropout $= 0.3$

View Embeddings
$$z_v^{(i)} \;=\; \operatorname{GAT}\!\bigl(\tilde{G}_i,\; \tilde{x}_v\bigr) \;\in\; \mathbb{R}^{128}, \qquad i \in \{1, 2\}$$
4
NT-Xent Contrastive Loss

Pulls together embeddings of the same node across the two views, while pushing apart embeddings of different nodes.

Cosine Similarity
$$\operatorname{sim}(\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}) \;=\; \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{a}\|\,\|\mathbf{b}\|}$$
NT-Xent Loss
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{con}} \;=\; -\frac{1}{|V|} \sum_{v \in V} \log \frac{ \exp\!\bigl(\operatorname{sim}(z_v^{(1)},\, z_v^{(2)}) \,/\, \tau\bigr) }{ \displaystyle\sum_{u \neq v} \exp\!\bigl(\operatorname{sim}(z_v^{(1)},\, z_u^{(2)}) \,/\, \tau\bigr) }$$

Temperature $\tau = 0.3$

5
BPR Ranking Loss

Bayesian Personalized Ranking: enforces that known cancer drivers score higher than non-driver genes.

Gene Score
$$s_v \;=\; \operatorname{MLP}(z_v) \;\in\; \mathbb{R}$$
BPR Loss (positive node $p$, negative node $q$)
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{rank}} \;=\; -\sum_{(p,\, q)} \log \sigma(s_p - s_q)$$

$\sigma$: sigmoid function  ·  Positive set: COSMIC CGC known cancer drivers

6
Joint Training Objective
Combined Loss
$$\mathcal{L} \;=\; (1 - \lambda)\;\mathcal{L}_{\text{rank}} \;+\; \lambda\;\mathcal{L}_{\text{con}}, \qquad \lambda = 0.2$$

Ranking loss carries $80\%$ of the signal; contrastive loss provides structural regularization at $20\%$.

7
EMA Parameter Update (Inference Stability)
Exponential Moving Average
$$\theta_{\text{EMA}} \;\leftarrow\; \gamma\;\theta_{\text{EMA}} \;+\; (1 - \gamma)\;\theta$$

$\theta$: current model parameters  ·  EMA weights used for scoring at inference time (not during training).

★
Evaluation Metric — NDCG@K

Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain: rewards ranking true cancer drivers near the top of the list, with a logarithmic position discount. Early stopping monitors NDCG@50 on validation nodes.

NDCG@K
$$\text{NDCG@}K \;=\; \frac{1}{Z} \sum_{k=1}^{K} \frac{2^{\,\mathbb{1}[v_k \in \mathcal{P}]} - 1}{\log_2(k + 1)}$$

$\mathcal{P}$: positive (CGC driver) node set  ·  $Z$: ideal DCG (all positives ranked first)  ·  Best fold: NDCG@50 $\approx 0.52$, AUROC $\approx 0.81$
